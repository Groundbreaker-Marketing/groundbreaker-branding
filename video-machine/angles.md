# Groundbreaker Marketing angles (belief bank)

Every ad tests one belief: what the buyer fears, values, blames, or wants control over. Build this
from the customer's own words first (sales calls, CRM notes, lost deals, reviews), quoted exactly
with the source, and only then from past ad results. AI sorts and drafts; it never invents a quote
or a claim. Each angle gets its own ad set and its own matched landing page.

Status: untested | testing | won | killed (kill at 3x target CPA; judge by theme average, never one ad)
