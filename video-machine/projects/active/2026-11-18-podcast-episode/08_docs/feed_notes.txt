Kim Seipp joins me to talk about why the best septic pros never stop learning. Kim runs the education program at the National Association of Wastewater Technicians (NAWT), and she and her husband own High Plains Sanitation Service in Colorado, a family septic business his father started in 1959.

A lot of you tell me customers call in a panic and you're never sure whether to roll the truck. Kim shares how she talks them off the cliff, why a quick photo can save you a trip, and why answering the phone still wins the job. We also get into how NAWT grew from a voice for septic pumpers into a training group, what a national class gives you that state training doesn't, and why she tells every installer and pumper to value their work.
