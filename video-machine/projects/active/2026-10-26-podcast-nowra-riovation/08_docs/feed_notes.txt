I recorded this one live at the NOWRA Mega-Conference with Stephen Moeller of RioVation. Stephen runs the business side of the company, and Gig Drewery, who developed the product, handles R&D and engineering. RioVation's BioMaze drops pods and ceramic diffusers into an existing septic tank and turns it into an aerobic tank.

Stephen explains how clean, class one effluent can bring a clogged drain field back, what the job costs (he puts it in the $5,000 range), how a contractor installs it, and how a 24 unit townhome complex and two big projects in Liberia and Ghana came out. You will see footage from the Liberia and Ghana installs and their BMT63HS aerator demo in the episode. The installs are not online anywhere, but RioVation's YouTube channel has more: https://www.youtube.com/@riovation

What we cover:
- What RioVation does and how the BioMaze got started
- Why treated water can bring a failing drain field back
- How the pods, diffusers and attached growth work
- When it fits a failing system, and what it costs
- How installers get started, how long an install takes and why maintenance agreements matter
- A $25,000 mound replacement that cost about $5,000 to fix instead
- The Liberia and Ghana projects
- Design help, commercial jobs and odor control
- How long the parts last
- Stephen's advice: be willing to try new technology
