My friend Kim Turner of Turner Septic Services joins me this week. Five years ago she had never pumped a septic tank. Her now-husband said they should start a septic company ("and by us, I mean you"), so she got her CDL and her New Hampshire septic inspector's license and started with one used truck, in an area where four family-owned haulers had been working for 30 years. Today her company has nearly 400 reviews and is growing fast.

Kim is honest about what this business really takes: the nights she cried in her truck, why she calls herself the bottleneck, and how education and humor built both her following and her reputation.

What we cover:
- Learning the trade from a mentor who would watch but not teach
- Turning septic education into funny TikToks (about 42,000 followers)
- The lonely side of owning a business, and why you have to ask for help
- Hiring friends and family, brand-new trucks and Starlink on every truck
- Pumping through a winter with frost 4 to 5 feet deep
- Why her inspection reports run 15 to 20 pages
- Using Claude to turn her inspection report into a checklist for her operations manager
